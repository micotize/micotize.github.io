My research concerns partial differential equations and mathematical fluid dynamics, with particular emphasis on mixing and enhanced dissipation, stability and instability of fluid flows, stochastic PDEs, active matter, and dynamo theory. My publication profiles are available on MathSciNet, Google Scholar, and arXiv. The arXiv versions may not always be the final published versions.
